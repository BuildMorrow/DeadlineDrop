# Product

## Vision and audience

Make transferring scheduling information into a calendar simple, free, and private for students, professionals, job applicants, and anyone managing personal events worldwide.

## Core journey

A user supplies content, sees potential deadlines, reviews and edits them, removes unwanted results, and exports selected events to a calendar file.

## Requirements

For v0.1, accept pasted English and Vietnamese text; detect potential dates, times and events; expose uncertainty and ambiguity; allow title, date and time corrections and removal; support all-day and timed events; and export selected reviewed events as ICS. Never silently invent missing information. Review is required before export.

Non-functional requirements: local processing, no mandatory paid AI APIs, accessibility, responsive design, maintainability, and static hosting without a backend, database or authentication.

## MVP boundaries

The v0.1 MVP is text input only. An experimental local image OCR workflow is under development for v0.2; PDF input, checklists and optional AI remain future work. Extraction remains limited and deterministic.

## Success criteria

Users can review a representative set of English and Vietnamese examples, correct uncertain results, and import the exported calendar events with their intended dates and times. Usability, accessibility, privacy and extraction quality should be evaluated with real examples before release.
